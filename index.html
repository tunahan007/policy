Decision Maker – Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Decision Maker app (Android & iOS) developed by Tahir Taban (FocusSoftware).

Data Collection

We do not collect any personally identifiable information. No user accounts, no login required. The decisions and options you enter are stored only on your device and are never transmitted to any server.

Advertising (AdMob)

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain information to show relevant ads, including your device's advertising identifier. We request only non-personalized ads. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

The app uses Google AdMob (advertising). No other third-party services are used for data collection or processing.

Age Restrictions

The app does not contain age-restricted content and is suitable for all age groups.

Changes to This Policy

If our privacy policy changes, we will update this page accordingly.
