Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how English Learning Guru ("the App") handles your information. The App is operated as an independent project and is available on Google Play.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information on external servers. All data generated by your use of the App (learning progress, quiz results, streak counts, and preferences) is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage and is never sent to us or any third party.

2. Advertising

The App may display banner advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain device identifiers and usage data in order to serve relevant ads, in accordance with Google's own privacy policy. You can review Google's data practices at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Speech / Audio

The App uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine (expo-speech) to pronounce English words and sentences. No audio is recorded or transmitted. The feature operates entirely on-device.

4. Permissions

  • Internet – required to load advertisements via AdMob.
  • No microphone, camera, location, or contacts permission is requested or used.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

All app data is stored locally on your device. You can delete all data at any time by uninstalling the App.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at the email address listed on the App's Google Play Store page.
